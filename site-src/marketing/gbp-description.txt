Custom Electrics Ltd is a City & Guilds qualified domestic electrician based in Pudsey, covering Leeds, Bradford and the surrounding parts of West Yorkshire.

I handle everything from a single socket to a full rewire: fuse board and consumer unit upgrades, full and partial rewires, EICRs and landlord electrical safety certificates, EV charger installation, fault finding and repairs, extra sockets, indoor and outdoor lighting, and power to garages, sheds, hot tubs and garden offices.

It is a one-man business, so the person who quotes your job is the person who turns up to do it. You get a fixed price agreed before work starts, full certification, and Building Control notification on notifiable work. No job too small.
