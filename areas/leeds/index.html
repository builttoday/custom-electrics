Leeds-specific work

Leeds has an enormous private rented sector, and with it a constant demand for EICRs. Student housing around Headingley and Hyde Park is generally HMO, which brings tighter requirements — interlinked alarms, emergency lighting in some cases, and inspection intervals that HMO licensing conditions can set shorter than the standard five years. Worth checking your licence rather than assuming five.

City centre apartments are a different problem again: landlord-owned risers, management company permissions and no easy route for new cable. Some things simply cannot be done in a leasehold flat without freeholder consent, and I would rather tell you that on the phone than after charging you for a visit.
